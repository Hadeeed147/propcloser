How it works

Say what you need

Write it the way you'd say it: "dedh crore", "kal 3 baje", "park ke paas". English and Roman Urdu both work, and you won't have to repeat yourself next time.

See homes that fit

The assistant searches this agency's list and nothing else. If no home matches, it says so and shows the closest ones instead of inventing one.

Check the monthly cost

Ask for an installment on any price. Unless you say otherwise it assumes 20% down, 10 years and 12% a year. It is an estimate, not a bank's offer.

Say yes to a visit

It holds a time and asks you first. Nothing is booked until you say yes, and you can move or cancel the visit in the same chat.

Good to know

What does it remember about me?

Your name and what you're looking for, such as area and budget. Never phone, CNIC or card numbers. You can see it or delete it at any time.

Are these homes and photos real?

No. PropCloser is a student project. The homes and prices are sample data, the photos are illustrations, and map pins show the area rather than an address.

Will it message me on its own?

No. It only replies to you. If it thinks a follow-up would help, it writes a draft, and a person at the agency approves or rejects it.

Can it be wrong?

Yes, it is AI. Prices and visit times come straight from the agency's records, but check the details that matter to you at the visit.
